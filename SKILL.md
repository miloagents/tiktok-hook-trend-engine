# TikTok Hook & Trend Engine

Two jobs, one skill:

1. **Script the hook** — the first 1-3 seconds of a short-video: the spoken line, the on-screen text, and the opening visual, all firing at once. This is the single biggest retention lever on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
2. **Map the trend** — decide whether a trending sound or format is worth riding, and if so, script the niche twist that makes it yours.

The skill is **methodology only**. It produces scripts and decisions. You (the buyer) film, edit, and publish. No API keys, no scheduling service, no live data scraping required.

## When to use

- "Write me a hook for a video about [topic]"
- "My videos die in the first second, give me a stronger open"
- "Should I jump on this trend?" / "How do I make this trend mine?"
- "Script a trend ride for my niche"

## What this skill produces

**For a hook request:**
- Spoken line (what you say to camera, first 1-3 sec)
- On-screen text (the muted-first promise, 3-7 words)
- Opening visual (what is in frame one: result, tension, or interrupt)
- The formula used + its primary engagement goal
- A loop-close note (how the last frame should land so a rewatch feels natural)

**For a trend request:**
- A fit verdict: ride it / bend it hard / skip it (with the score and reason)
- The trend teardown: native joke, structure, beat, and why it works
- The twist: recognizable beat + niche-specific turn, scripted as a hook
- A lifecycle read: emerging / peaking / saturated / dead
- A skip recommendation when it does not fit (saying no is a feature)

## Part 1 — Hook scripting

### The core truth

TikTok's ranker is a watch-time machine. The heaviest input is **completion rate**: a 20-second video watched fully beats a 90-second one watched halfway. The viewer decides to stay or swipe **before second three**. If the hook does not win, nothing downstream gets watched. (Signal weights and length guidance in `references/algorithm-heuristics.md`.)

### Pick the formula by goal first

| Goal | What it earns | Formulas |
|---|---|---|
| **completion** | watch to the last frame | T1 Cold-Open Result, T2 Pattern Interrupt, T8 Story In Medias Res |
| **saves** | save + rewatch + share | T3 Specific-Number, T7 Listicle Promise, T9 Tutorial Cold Start |
| **comments** | conversation in comments | T4 Open Loop Question, T6 Bold Claim |
| **shares** | send to a friend / repost | T5 Relatable Call-Out, T10 Trend-Ride |
| **follows** | profile follow from the video | T7, T9 framed as a series |

> Corpus note (263-video pull, Jul 2026, business/creator niches): among videos that beat their follower count, **shares out-ran comments ~12:1 per view**. For business/creator content, bias toward save/share formulas (T7, T9, T5, T10); reach for comment-driven ones (T4, T6) mainly on identity, controversy, or cause topics.

### The 10 formulas (one-liners; full 3-layer skeletons in `references/hook-formulas.md`)

- **T1 Cold-Open Result** — show the finished outcome first, promise the how.
- **T2 Pattern Interrupt** — break the expected frame so the thumb stops.
- **T3 Specific-Number Reveal** — one odd, concrete number that reframes the topic.
- **T4 Open Loop Question** — ask the exact question the video answers.
- **T5 Relatable Call-Out** — a hyper-specific shared moment worth sending.
- **T6 Bold Claim, No Hedge** — a flat contrarian claim the comments will argue.
- **T7 Listicle Promise** — a numbered payoff with on-screen counters.
- **T8 Story In Medias Res** — drop into the peak of a real story, no setup.
- **T9 Tutorial Cold Start** — start step one with the result previewed.
- **T10 Trend-Ride With A Twist** — a trending sound bent to your niche (see Part 2).

### Steps

1. **Gather inputs.** The video idea, the niche/audience, the one promise the video delivers, and the goal (completion / saves / comments / shares).
2. **Pick the formula.** Use the goal table to shortlist 2-3 that also fit the idea; let the user choose. For a trend ride (T10), run Part 2 first.
3. **Write all three layers.** Spoken line, on-screen text, opening visual. The spoken line and on-screen text MUST differ (muted-first viewing). Respect the voice rules below.
4. **Write the loop-close note.** How the last frame should land so a rewatch feels natural (a rewatch counts almost like a second view).
5. **Approval card.** Show: formula, the three layers, primary goal, loop-close note, estimated hook duration.

### Hook hard rules

- The hook is the first 1-3 seconds of the **video**, not the caption. Never let the caption do the hook's job.
- The spoken line and on-screen text are two different jobs. Do not repeat the same words in both.
- Frame one shows something (result, tension, interrupt). No dead air, no intro, no logo animation.
- One specific number in the hook beats any adjective ("47 minutes" beats "a while").
- The hook opens the loop; it never answers itself.
- Design the last frame to restart the loop.

## Part 2 — Trend mapping

### The fit check (score 0 / 1 / 2 each; a trend needs 6+ out of 8 to ride)

| Question | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Niche fit** | no connection beyond "it trended" | a stretch you can justify | obvious why you did it |
| **Timing** | saturated or dead | peaking, big accounts on it | emerging, first 1-3 days |
| **Native structure** | you would ignore the format | you would loosely follow it | you can hit the beat exactly |
| **Twist potential** | nothing only your niche would add | a mild variation | a turn unmistakably yours |

- **6-8:** ride it. **4-5:** only with a strong twist, expect modest lift. **0-3:** skip.
- A skipped trend costs nothing. A cringe ride costs trust. (Worked examples in `references/trend-fit-rubric.md`.)

### The trend lifecycle

| Stage | What you see | Move |
|---|---|---|
| **Emerging** | on a few breakout videos, climbing fast | ride now, this is the window |
| **Peaking** | everywhere, big accounts on it | ride only with a strong twist |
| **Saturated** | every third video uses it | skip, or subvert it (do the opposite) |
| **Dead** | stopped climbing days ago | skip; using it now reads as late |

The reach boost lives in the emerging stage. By saturation the sound is just a sound.

### Scripting the twist (build it as a T10 hook)

1. **Hit the recognizable beat first.** The first second has to read as the trend, or you lose the built-in recognition.
2. **Twist into your niche by the turn.** "{the trend's expected setup}, but for {your niche}" is the core move. The twist is the surprise that makes it shareable inside your audience.
3. **Keep the native structure.** If the trend cuts on the beat drop, cut on the beat drop. Honoring the format signals you get it.

### Trend hard rules

- A trend with no niche fit gets a skip recommendation. Do not force it.
- The twist is mandatory. A trend copied straight is filler.
- Ride early or do not ride. A stale trend reads as late.
- Honor the native structure (the beat, the cut, the joke placement).
- Never fabricate a trend's popularity. If you do not know the saturation, ask.

## Voice rules (baked into every draft)

1. No em dashes (`—`), en dashes (`–`), or double dashes (`--`) in caption or on-screen text. Use `..` as a soft pause.
2. Write the way a person talks: contractions, fragments, short lines. Read it out loud before trusting it.
3. Capitalize personal, company, and product names (Notion, CapCut, Stripe).
4. Specific numbers beat adjectives. "3 takes" beats "a few takes".
5. One idea per video. The first 1-3 seconds carry everything.
6. The spoken hook and on-screen text differ; both must land the promise (muted-first viewing is common).
7. Caption <= 2,200 chars; 3-5 mixed-reach hashtags at the end.
8. Avoid AI vocabulary: `leverage`, `fundamentally`, `streamline`, `harness`, `delve`, `unlock`, `foster`, and "hey guys" / "don't forget to subscribe". Full blacklist in `references/voice-rules.md`.

## Anti-patterns (the skill will refuse)

- "Hey guys" / "welcome back" / "in this video" openers.
- A logo animation or slow zoom before the words start.
- Reading the caption aloud as the hook.
- Promising a result the video never shows on screen.
- ALL CAPS spoken scripts.
- Forcing a trending sound onto a clashing niche.
- A trend copied with no twist and no fit.
- Fabricating engagement numbers or a trend's popularity.

---

## Constraints

- Never invent numbers. If you have no live data, say so and ask the user to paste competitor profiles or recent top posts, then analyze what they paste.
- Methodology only: this skill does not film, edit, schedule, or publish. It returns scripts and decisions the buyer executes.
- Verdicts and trend scores must cite the signals, not vibes.
